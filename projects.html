Research Themes

What Fuels the Tree of Life? Understanding the Drivers of Diversification

Diversification • Speciation Rates • Macroevolution • Biogeography

Why do some groups of organisms explode in diversity while others remain species-poor? This research investigates how the origin of new species varies across lineages, regions, and climates. By combining phylogenetic, ecological, and climatic approaches, I explore the evolutionary forces that govern the diversity of life on Earth.

Causes of Species Richness Patterns Across Space, Time, and Clades

Species Richness • Biodiversity Gradients • Macroecology • Biogeography

From tropical forests to polar regions, biodiversity is unevenly distributed across the planet. This research explores why some regions harbour thousands of species while others support only a few. By integrating ecological, evolutionary, and historical perspectives, I investigate the processes that generate and maintain global patterns of species richness.

Evolution and Function of Animal Coloration

Mammalian Diversity • Trait Evolution • Adaptation • Functional Ecology

From camouflage to communication, coloration plays many roles in the lives of animals. This research investigates how colour patterns evolve across mammals and what ecological and evolutionary forces shape their diversity. By studying the origins and functions of mammalian coloration, I aim to understand how traits evolve in response to changing environments and lifestyles.
